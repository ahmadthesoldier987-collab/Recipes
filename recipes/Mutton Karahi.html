Mutton Karahi

Back to Recipes

Description:

Mutton Karahi is a traditional Pakistani and North Indian dish made by cooking tender pieces of mutton with tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and aromatic spices. It is prepared in a deep, thick pan called a karahi, which gives the dish its name. Known for its rich, spicy flavor and thick tomato-based gravy, Mutton Karahi is often served with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg mutton, cut into pieces
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Fresh ginger, cut into thin strips
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed

Instructions:

Heat the oil or ghee in a large karahi over medium heat. Add the mutton pieces and cook until they begin to brown. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute before adding the chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin seeds, black pepper, and salt. Cook until the tomatoes soften and blend into a thick gravy. Add a little water, cover the karahi, and let the mutton simmer on low heat until it becomes tender. Once the meat is fully cooked, sprinkle in the garam masala, add the sliced green chilies, ginger strips, and chopped coriander leaves, then cook for a few more minutes. Serve the Mutton Karahi hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.
