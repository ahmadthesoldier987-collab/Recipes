Beef Pulao

Back to Recipes

Description:

Beef Pulao is a flavorful rice dish made with tender beef, fragrant basmati rice, and a blend of aromatic spices. Unlike biryani, the rice and meat are cooked together, allowing the rice to absorb the rich flavors of the beef broth and spices. It is a comforting and satisfying meal that is commonly enjoyed with raita, salad, or pickles.

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg beef, cut into cubes
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed

Instructions:

Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 30 minutes. Heat the oil or ghee in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Add the cumin seeds, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon stick, and bay leaves, then cook until fragrant. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and add the beef, cooking until it is lightly browned. Season with black pepper, garam masala, and salt, then add enough water to cover the beef and simmer until the meat is tender. Add the soaked rice to the beef along with the appropriate amount of broth or water. Cook until the rice has absorbed the liquid, then reduce the heat, cover the pot tightly, and let the pulao steam for 15–20 minutes. Gently fluff the rice with a fork, garnish with fresh coriander leaves, and serve hot with raita or a fresh salad.
