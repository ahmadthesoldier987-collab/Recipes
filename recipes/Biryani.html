Biryani

Back to Recipes

Description:

Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made by layering fragrant basmati rice with spiced meat or vegetables. It is cooked with aromatic spices such as cumin, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon, giving it a rich flavor and irresistible aroma. Biryani is commonly served on special occasions, family gatherings, and celebrations.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 2 teaspoons biryani masala
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Fresh mint leaves, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed

Instructions:

Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 30 minutes. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Add the whole spices, followed by the ginger-garlic paste, and cook until fragrant. Stir in the chicken, tomatoes, yogurt, green chilies, biryani masala, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt, then cook until the chicken is tender. Meanwhile, boil the rice until it is about 70% cooked and drain it. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken mixture, sprinkle with chopped coriander and mint leaves, cover the pot tightly, and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes. Once fully cooked, gently mix the biryani and serve it hot with raita or a fresh salad.
